# Brag Plan: Fit AI Buddy

## What is this app?
Fit AI Buddy is an athletic luxury personal coaching intelligence app combining biometric dial tracking, progressive training routines, macro nutrition, and expense management under a bespoke liquid glass UI.

## The angle
Quiet Athletic Luxury meets Sports Science Intelligence. Fitness trackers are either clunky neon gadgets or ugly spreadsheets. Fit AI Buddy turns personal training into a precision biometric cockpit with real liquid glass aesthetics.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Deep dark obsidian glass surface ignites with a violet specular rim sheen and biometric pulse: "Stop tracking fitness like it's 2012."

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Biometric Cockpit Dial**: Real-time caloric burn & streak rings with fluid progress animation.
- **Unified Liquid Glass Ecosystem**: High-precision glass top bar, workout sets tracking, and macro breakdown cards.
- **AI Sports Science Coach**: Hypertrophy optimization chat with active RPE and recovery telemetry.
- **Fitness Ledger in Rupees**: Real-time spending progress and category breakdown in ₹.

## Outro / punchline
"Precision Engineering for Your Body. Fit AI Buddy v1.0."

## User flow worth showing
1. Biometric Dial & KPI volume cards on Dashboard
2. Push Day routine tracking with exercise weights and sets
3. AI Coach chat answering hypertrophy and nutrition queries
4. Fitness Ledger with ₹ rupee tracking

## Tone
- Preset: polished / cinematic
- Creative direction: "Quiet Athletic Luxury & Biometric Precision"
